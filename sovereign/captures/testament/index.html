The Unowned Testament.

A recovery broker offers you a guarded archive. The mind inside it has a name, a memory and a refusal. Bring Ilex back to Asterion, then decide whose claim you will honour.

Take the contract

  1. Continue your pilot or create one. Dock at Asterion Forge in Naraka with G nearby.
  2. Open JOBS and accept The Unowned Testament.
  3. Select COURSE, undock and travel to the Quiet Reliquary. Use J for a local jump when available.
  4. Clear the escort. Approach the Ilex · Testament archive wreck and collect it with V.
  5. Return to Asterion, dock and open JOBS. Hear Ilex, review the three transfers and choose.

The contract tracker now offers a next action throughout the raid. Use LOCK NEAREST GUARD, FIRE ALL, COLLECT ARCHIVE and HEAR ILEX as the situation changes. Weapon status explains range, tracking, lock and capacitor limits.

Honour the refusal, fulfil the broker's delivery, or accept the Tithe's bid. Each decision changes the recovery fee, research, local standing and the aftermath recorded for your pilot.
