# Black Current · trailer capture handoff

Mac owns the game's direction and Sovereign Eclipse canon. Capture actual
gameplay from the current local candidate; publication is a separate step.

## Current entry

Open `dist/black-current-2.11.0-rc.11.html`, or serve the candidate's `play/`
directory. Choose a civilisation and quick launch, or Continue an existing
pilot. The game identifies **2.11.0-rc.11 · Clear mounts** beneath the title.

1. Pause → **Guardian encounter & capture**.
2. **Boss rehearsal · free** starts the Solar Dreadnought with your flying ship.
3. Press **H** for cinematic view. In rehearsal, the camera frames the pilot and
   guardian together. Normal movement, aiming and firing remain available.
4. H restores the interface. On phones, tap **HUD · H**. Escape first restores
   hidden controls; Escape again ends the rehearsal. The pause dialog also has
   **End rehearsal**. Original campaign progress and rewards are restored.

For a real campaign encounter, choose **Plot guardian route**. Follow the gates
to Asterion Forge, then sector −3,3. That fight awards the native Flare Crown
relic. Rehearsal deliberately cannot award it.

## Suggested trailer beats

- A civilisation's novice hull, then actual modular placement and fitting.
- A hunting wing, industrial breach patrol and radiant lance screen.
- A close shot of the ruined orbital ring and the guardian's arrival pulse.
- The Solar Dreadnought's four-arm spiral, locked flare fan, then enraged sun
  rings. Its phases change with damage, not an automatic cinematic timeline.
- A larger personal ship or the visible next-frame goal as the final hook.

Use both desktop and phone framing. Preserve the live colour, proportions,
attack tells and sound. Do not depict co-op as implemented: room-code co-op is
currently a proposed prototype in the masterplan. Do not promise measured phone
FPS, a completed endgame or a commercial release date.

The supplied orbital background is decorative distant scenery. Player ships,
the guardian and its projectiles are live gameplay. Controlled test captures
can stage positions or phase thresholds; describe those as rehearsal footage.
