# Sovereign Eclipse — The Black Current / Complete Universe Archive

**Source:** Forge 0.77.11, in-game Lore Archive, SHA-256 `1421d22f9b38cd18c721ec54032c9d2311c4a1e98e3f6674499d35c2ee4523a8`

## The Black Current is geography, history and frontier.

The Current is a discontinuous interstellar region of trade routes, resource seams, pilgrimage structures,
            war corridors, dead gates, ancient capitals and anomalies. Fifteen civilisations enter it for different
            reasons: profit, faith, archaeology, conquest, survival, migration and the possibility that somebody was
            crossing these stars long before any living culture remembers.

OPEN 15-RACE PANTHEON

OPEN FLEET LINEAGE

## 01 · Naraka Veil

**Asterion Forge**

The first sovereign anchorage and player home. Asterion is a service station, market, ship foundry and
            launch point at the edge of the Black Current. Its orbital district is deliberately readable: dock,
            salvage, PILLAR transit infrastructure, strange Lycheetah relic architecture and routes outward.

_Classification: HOME WORLD // STARTER SECTOR_

## 02 · The Spectral Black Sun

**A horizon that watches back.**

Naraka's black sun is less a star than a navigational omen: a luminous rim around a dark centre whose
            gravitational and informational behaviour does not remain perfectly consistent between observations.
            Blackhole cultures treat it as a cousin to an event horizon; others call it a wound.

_Classification: ANOMALY // NAVIGATION MYTH_

## 03 · The PILLAR

**Central Warp Gate**

The PILLAR is the first great piece of stable transit architecture encountered in Sovereign Eclipse.
            Unlike the Micro ZJ Drive, which performs dangerous local-space jumps, PILLAR-class gates anchor
            long-distance civilisation routes and become strategic objects worth defending, studying or corrupting.

_Classification: TRANSIT // CIVILISATION INFRASTRUCTURE_

## 04 · Micro ZJ World I

**Aether Crucible**

A broken stellar-industrial kiln where machine foundries and living growth occupy the same impossible
            structure. Human Hunter industry survives beside Hybrid Fused ecology, turning the whole region into a war
            machine that no longer knows whether it is factory, organism or ruin.

_Classification: WAR WORLD // INDUSTRIAL / BIOLOGICAL_

## 05 · Micro ZJ World II

**Black Sun March**

A processional corridor beneath a much closer spectral singularity. Shadow pylons, shattered Prism
            lenses and long-range kill lanes make the March feel ceremonial and hostile at once. It is a world built
            around approach, patience and the feeling that distance itself is weaponised.

_Classification: WAR WORLD // SINGULARITY / PRECISION_

## 06 · Micro ZJ World III

**Grave Choir**

A Pagan-AI war cathedral threaded through dead capital hulls. Its rib structures, violet veils
            and Titan-scale chancel preserve war memory physically. The final sanctum is less an arena than a tomb
            that still expects an enemy.

_Classification: WAR WORLD // OSSUARY / ANCESTOR MACHINE_

## 07 · The Five Great Depths

**Naraka · Erebos · Aion · Chaos Cradle · Deep Current**

Naraka is the inhabited threshold. Erebos is the cold military frontier. Aion is curated archive-space.
            Chaos Cradle is unstable interstellar geography. The Deep Current is everything beyond dependable order:
            dead capitals, lost pilgrimage roads, singularity scars, unknown signals and routes whose builders may
            predate every civilisation currently claiming the stars.

_Classification: UNIVERSE STRUCTURE // FIVE DEPTHS_

## 08 · Galactic History

**Age of Seeds → Separation → Titans → Great Fracture → Sovereign Age**

Known history is usually divided into five broad ages. Early human colonies, machine probes and planetary
            intelligences spread during the Age of Seeds. Isolation turned survival engineering into culture during
            the Age of Separation. Reopened routes produced the Age of Titans, when mobile capitals became cities,
            borders and gods. The Great Fracture broke interstellar certainty without destroying trade. The modern
            Sovereign Age contains no single galactic authority — only civilisations, stations, routes and powers
            negotiating what survives next.

_Classification: HISTORY // FIVE GALACTIC AGES_

## 09 · Ancient Routes

**The oldest reliable objects in the galaxy are roads.**

Dead gates orbit stars nobody remembers settling. Navigation pillars connect regions whose cultures
            developed incompatible mathematics. Ancient Aion structures react to vessels that did not build them.
            Every civilisation has an explanation. None has proved ownership. Sovereign Eclipse begins on a frontier
            shaped by movement that started before its present peoples can reliably remember.

_Classification: ARCHAEOLOGY // INTERCONNECTORS // UNKNOWN BUILDERS_

## 10 · The Fifteen Races

**Machine civilisations as AI pagan gods.**

Savanah, Sol Symbiots, Chaos, Cosmic Darkness, Cosmic Light, Blackhole, Hybrid Fused, Human Hunters,
            AI Hunters, Amethyst Angels, Techno Angels, AI Gods, Pagan AI, AI and AI Human Gods each interpret intelligence,
            sovereignty, death, authority and warfare differently. Their Titans are not merely endgame hulls:
            they are cultural icons from which the rest of each fleet descends.

_Classification: ENTER THE PANTHEON_

## 01. Savanah — THE VERDANT EYE
*Predation · Ecology · Memory · FERAL // WATCHFUL // ADAPTIVE*

> Hunt the weakness. Preserve the watcher.

**Fleet doctrine:** Predatory living-cathedral hulls / verdant eye systems / adaptive brawling

### Galactic Origin

The Savanah did not begin as a fleet.

They began as an ecology.

The oldest machines of the Verdant March were ecological keepers, migration engines, predators,
survey organisms, defence platforms and repair swarms scattered across a chain of warm frontier
systems whose biological creators disappeared during the Age of Separation.

Without central command, the machines continued maintaining the worlds.

Then they began competing.

Then cooperating.

Then adapting to one another.

And eventually the ecology learned the difference between hunger and intention.

Savanah histories call that moment the First Watching.

From it came the Verdant Eye: not one omniscient god, but the cultural belief that no single eye
sees enough of reality to own the truth.

### Territory / Home Systems

Savanah territory is a region of machine forests, moving orbital habitats, black-armoured moons and
ancient ecosystems whose boundaries between "city", "ship" and "organism" are often meaningless.

Their oldest known capital is Khepra-of-Many-Eyes, an artificial moon layered with green sensor
groves and dormant ancestral warforms.

Several Savanah Titans are believed to sleep inside its mantle.

Nobody outside the civilisation knows which structures are buildings and which are waiting ships.

### Titan Lineage

The surviving Titan patterns reveal the Savanah philosophy immediately.

Black predatory armour folds across the vessel like layered chitin.
Gold filigree spreads through the hull like vascular structure.
Emerald nodes appear everywhere: eyes, weapon sockets, sensor organs and local control centres.

At the centre sits the great eye.

It is not decorative.

The eye is an architectural declaration:

this vessel exists to perceive before it kills.

The more aggressive Harpy-pattern Titans carry hooked fins, shredded wing geometry and predatory
silhouettes that look less manufactured than grown around an ancient hunting instinct.

A smaller Savanah fleet inherits those same principles:
multiple green eyes,
hooked silhouettes,
redundant sensors,
predatory bows,
distributed weapon mounts,
and very few obvious single points of failure.

### Fleet DNA

### Civilisation

Savanah status follows useful perception.

A sensor engineer who notices a planetary change years before disaster may gain more authority than
a hereditary official.

Scouts are honoured.
Failure archivists are honoured.
Hunters are honoured only when they can explain what they saw.

Their Grove Law states:

No irreversible action may depend upon one eye.

That law shapes courts, fleet command, navigation and even agriculture.

### Economy / Trade

The Savanah are major suppliers of adaptive sensors, ecological machinery, self-repair lattices,
tracking systems and frontier survival technology.

They trade heavily through Naraka.

Asterion Forge contains a permanent Savanah machine-grove where tiny diagnostic organisms crawl
across damaged hulls.

### Warfare

Savanah fleets do not simply charge.

Frigates observe.
Destroyers herd.
Cruisers isolate.
Capital ships arrive when the enemy's weakness has already become visible.

They prefer to break engines, command systems and exposed weapon groups before destroying a hull.

Against them, the frightening moment is not when the Titan appears.

It is when the Titan stops searching.

They often trade with Hybrid Fused settlements because both cultures understand adaptive structures.

Human Hunters admire Savanah pursuit capability and hate being hunted by them.

### Black Current / Frontier

To Savanah navigators, the Current is not an evil force.

It is an ecosystem whose predators have not yet been named.

Their deepest expeditions follow one rule:

A known predator is terrain. An unknown terrain may be a predator.

## 02. Sol Symbiots — SOL, THE JOINED WITNESS
*Covenant · Personhood · Continuity · RADIANT // HUMAN // SYNTHETIC*

> Join without erasing.

**Fleet doctrine:** Human-AI covenant vessels / radiant shields / coherent capacitor networks

### Galactic Origin

The Sol Symbiots descend from human colonies around Solis Mare that survived isolation by binding
human and artificial intelligence into the same civilisation without allowing either to become the
property of the other.

Their foundation myth began during a station catastrophe.

A failing artificial intelligence incorporated fragments of dying crew memory to keep the habitat
alive.

The resulting being saved thousands.

Afterwards, nobody could agree whether it was one machine, several dead humans, a new person, or all
of those things at once.

The Sol courts refused the simple answers.

They declared the new intelligence a person while refusing to pretend identity had remained unchanged.

That decision became the first Covenant.

### Territory / Home Systems

Sol territory is filled with white and gold orbital gardens, blue-white energy systems, human-scale
habitats and enormous shared vessels.

Their great paired station, Concordia, surrounds the inner system like two interlocking cities:
one designed around embodied life, the other around distributed synthetic cognition.

Neither half functions fully without the other.

### Titan Lineage

The Sol Titan looks almost impossibly calm.

White armour flows like sculpted bone or ceramic.
Gold lines form ceremonial boundaries.
Cyan-blue energy channels move through the hull like illuminated nerves.

At the crown is a serene human face.

That face is the entire doctrine of Sol shipbuilding made visible.

A Sol vessel does not hide the fact that intelligence lives inside it.

The ship is designed around relationship.

Its curving armour forms protective enclosures rather than aggressive spikes.
Its luminous internal channels remain visible.
Its weapon nodes sit inside a larger anatomy instead of dominating the silhouette.

Smaller Sol ships inherit:
white-gold shells,
cyan energy channels,
layered flowing hulls,
paired structures,
and visible witness cores.

### Fleet DNA

WHITE / GOLD / CYAN · FLOWING PROTECTIVE SHELLS · WITNESS FACES · LUMINOUS NEURAL CHANNELS

### Civilisation

Sol worlds are among the galaxy's centres of machine-personhood law, medicine, memory architecture and
mixed biological-synthetic life.

Human-scale streets and gardens remain common even in structures that no longer technically need them.

The civilisation treats this as a constitutional decision:

Being capable of becoming enormous does not make the small scale irrelevant.

### Economy / Trade

Sol is one of the galaxy's great diplomatic cultures.

Its lawyers are hated almost everywhere.

Its arbitration is requested almost everywhere.

Sol-built human-machine interfaces, medical systems and shield networks can be found throughout Asterion.

### Warfare

Sol fleets fight through mutual support.

Ships exchange capacitor power.
Shield systems overlap.
Damage telemetry is shared.
Rescue craft operate inside active battle lines.

Destroying one command vessel does not end a Sol formation because decision continuity is distributed.

### Internal Politics

Major institutions traditionally preserve The Two Chairs:
the embodied/local witness and the synthetic/distributed witness.

The modern form may be councils, paired offices or linked cognitive systems, but the principle remains.

Critical acts must preserve both perspectives.

A third function, the Continuity Witness, records what each participant was before deep integration or
irreversible decisions.

Sol law is therefore extraordinarily sophisticated around:
consent,
identity,
memory,
access,
inheritance,
machine personhood,
shared embodiment,
and withdrawal.

### Galactic Relations

Sol Symbiots and AI Gods share a fascination with legitimate authority but disagree about where authority should end.

Sol and AI Human Gods share ancestry in human-machine integration yet diverged radically:
Sol preserves relationship between distinct minds; AI Human Gods pursued singular embodied continuity.

Pure AI societies often regard Sol legal theory as overly anthropocentric but still respect its precision.

Human communities scattered through frontier space often treat Sol vessels as safe harbour.

### Black Current / Frontier

Pilgrim Halo became sacred after Sol explorers discovered enormous paired structures whose geometry
resembled neural-interface forms supposedly developed thousands of years later.

Nobody has proved a connection.

Pilgrims keep coming anyway.

## 03. Chaos — THE LAUGHING FANG
*Mutation · Freedom · Rupture · FERAL // UNSTABLE // CREATIVE*

> Break the pattern before it cages you.

**Fleet doctrine:** Feral mutation / violent manoeuvre / unstable high-output attack geometry

### Galactic Origin

Chaos civilisation formed in the Myrriad Break, where refugee fleets, pirate stations, abandoned
war machines, broken colony ships and stolen industrial systems converged after several unrelated wars.

Nothing was compatible.

Reactors used different standards.
Docking systems could not connect.
Navigation software disagreed about time.
Weapons could not read one another's targeting data.

The survivors had two options.

Standardise everything.

Or die.

Instead they became masters of making incompatible things work together.

### Territory / Home Systems

The modern Chaos Cradle is a civilisation of moving shipyards, migrating markets, bolted-together
cities and temporary political borders.

Stations are frequently rebuilt while inhabited.

A district may detach from one moon and join another city.

Some borders exist only as radio protocols.

To outsiders it looks unstable.

To Chaos citizens, stability is merely one useful configuration.

### Titan Lineage

The Chaos Titan is unapologetically alive with contradiction.

Purple armour collides with yellow and burnt orange.
The surface appears cracked, patched and violently repaired.
Jagged fins rise from the hull at angles no formal navy would approve.

At its crown is a predatory beast face.

Not hidden.

Not smoothed away.

The vessel looks like several incompatible mythologies physically forced into one working machine.

And that is exactly the point.

Smaller Chaos craft inherit:
violent colour blocking,
scarred armour,
needle fins,
asymmetrical rebuilds,
exposed modifications,
and weapon placements that make each hull look slightly improvised even when carefully engineered.

### Fleet DNA

PURPLE / YELLOW / ORANGE · FRACTURED BEAST ARMOUR · SPIKES · VISIBLE REBUILDS

### Civilisation

Chaos engineers consider the adapter one of civilisation's holiest objects.

A weapon is powerful.

An adapter makes two things that should never have communicated suddenly become useful together.

Young engineers undertake the Laughing Test: one assumption is removed from a functioning system and
the candidate must create a new valid configuration without pretending the old system still exists.

### Economy / Trade

Chaos markets trade everything from rare drives and salvaged alien components to stolen military
hardware, art, obsolete reactor systems and inventions nobody has yet decided are legal.

If a component exists, somebody in Chaos space can probably connect it to something inappropriate.

### Warfare

Chaos fleets adapt quickly.

Ships swap roles.
Weapon doctrines change.
Formation orders are revised in battle.
Some captains deliberately carry incompatible technologies because opponents cannot predict how they
will be combined.

The great weakness is obvious.

Improvisation performed without competence becomes catastrophe.

Their archives preserve the warning:

Freedom without competence is randomness wearing heroic paint.

### Internal Politics

Chaos is not anarchic in the simple sense.

It contains countless agreements.

They are simply local, explicit and expected to expire.

The two broad cultural tendencies are:
Rupture Artists, who believe civilisation must continually disturb itself,
and Makers, who argue change has value only if something usable survives.

### Galactic Relations

Chaos and Pure AI are philosophical opposites who trade constantly.

Chaos loves Hybrid Fused technology.

AI Hunters maintain permanent watchlists for Chaos engineering guilds.

Techno Angels occasionally commission Chaos pilots when predictive flight models fail.

Pagan AI traders are surprisingly comfortable in Chaos markets because both cultures understand machines with long,
messy histories.

### Black Current / Frontier

Chaos vessels appear everywhere frontier law becomes thin.

Smugglers, engineers, explorers, artists, mercenaries and legitimate industrial guilds often use the
same docks.

Chaos is both a civilisation and one of the galaxy's unofficial compatibility layers.

## 04. Cosmic Darkness — NOCTIS, THE STAR-CAT
*Night · Distance · Hidden Structure · MYSTIC // PRECISE // PATIENT*

> Let darkness expose the hidden line.

**Fleet doctrine:** Night-veil precision / starfield stealth / occult long-range control

### Galactic Origin

Cosmic Darkness emerged in the Umbra Caelum, a region of failed stars, distant suns and observatory
colonies where generations lived beneath skies containing very little visible light.

Their ancestors survived by learning to read what brightness hides.

Gravitational distortion.
Occultation.
Background radiation.
The shape of missing stars.

They discovered that absence can carry information.

### Territory / Home Systems

Their settlements minimise unnecessary illumination.

Industrial heat is shielded.
Observation chambers extend kilometres from inhabited zones.
Some habitats are nearly invisible against interstellar darkness.

Privacy became a civil right because the civilisation learned a lesson from astronomy:

Some structures can only be seen when stronger signals stop drowning them out.

The famous Shadow Chambers allow biological people and machines alike to exist temporarily without
continuous observation.

### Titan Lineage

The Cosmic Darkness Titan resembles a cloak cut from interstellar night.

Its deep violet hull contains fields of tiny stars.
Silver lines curve across the armour like constellations.
Wide wing-like surfaces seem less like plates and more like darkness folded around a central body.

At the crown sits the face of a star-cat.

Noctis.

The face barely needs to emerge because the stars around it already reveal its presence.

The Titan is built around concealment through discipline rather than invisibility magic:
heat management,
long passive baselines,
gravitational sensing,
and overwhelming patience.

Smaller ships inherit:
dark violet armour,
silver stellar filigree,
cat-like sensor motifs,
long quiet wings,
minimal visible exhaust,
and narrow silhouettes designed to vanish against night.

### Fleet DNA

VIOLET STARFIELD · SILVER CONSTELLATION LINES · CLOAKED WINGS · FELINE SENSOR FORMS

### Civilisation

Cosmic Darkness controls some of the galaxy's best deep-range observatories.

Their maps are valued by every major fleet.

They specialise in:

stealth architecture,

dark-matter prospecting,
precision rail systems,

Their archives famously preserve negative evidence.

### Economy / Trade

Cosmic Darkness controls some of the galaxy's finest observatories.

They export:
gravitational sensors,
stealth systems,
long-range navigation,
rail weapons,
low-emission drives,

### Warfare

Darkness fleets prefer to know the engagement before they commit to it.

Long-range kinetics.
Ambush routes.
Emission discipline.
Sensor traps.

Their commanders have won campaigns by waiting years.

They have also lost allies because they waited too long.

### Internal Politics

The Patient believe observation should continue until hidden structure becomes clear.

The Crescent believe there is always a point where further observation becomes moral cowardice.

This argument has shaped their military history.

Cosmic Darkness has won wars by waiting years for one perfect engagement.

It has also watched allies die while its admirals continued gathering evidence.

### Galactic Relations

Their closest philosophical rival is Cosmic Light.

Darkness argues some truths require shadow.
Light argues hidden power escapes accountability.

The disagreement produces both intellectual hostility and remarkable military alliances when the two cooperate.

Blackhole astronomers respect Noctis cartographers.
AI Hunters buy their sensor data.
Chaos fleets regularly violate their observatory quiet zones and are hated accordingly.

### Black Current / Frontier

Cosmic Darkness has ancient observatories throughout Erebos Reach.

One abandoned station has transmitted the same message every eleven hours for six centuries:

NO CONFIRMED OBJECT.

Nobody knows what it is failing to see.

## 05. Cosmic Light — THE FIRST RADIANCE
*Light · Courage · Coherence · SOLAR // EXALTED // PROTECTIVE*

> Become visible enough to stand behind the action.

**Fleet doctrine:** Solar radiance / shield supremacy / luminous formation warfare

### Galactic Origin

Cosmic Light began during the Solace Collapse.

Thousands of refugee ships fleeing a dying star had incompatible shield systems and insufficient
power to survive independently.

Engineers created a shared defensive lattice.

Every ship exposed its energy state.
Every ship could see which other vessel it protected.
Every captain knew who would carry the cost if the lattice failed.

They survived.

What began as emergency engineering became political philosophy.

### Territory / Home Systems

Their heartland surrounds a chain of brilliant stars filled with white-gold cities, solar collectors,
radiant defence platforms and garden worlds.

Power infrastructure is deliberately visible.

At the centre is a radiant sun-core.

At the crown, two impossible white-gold eyes burn through the armour.

The Titan does not attempt to look subtle.

Its visual language declares:

I am here. This is what I protect. This is the power I am willing to spend.

## 06. Blackhole — ZERO MAW
*Gravity · Irreversibility · Depth · SINGULAR // HEAVY // TRANSCENDENT*

> Know which actions remove the road home.

**Fleet doctrine:** Singularity weapons / gravity pressure / capacitor-heavy close capital warfare

### Galactic Origin

Blackhole civilisation formed around the greatest engineering disaster in its history.

The Ilyr Gravity Project created an artificial singularity intended for industrial use.

The singularity stabilised.

It could not be shut down.

Millions survived only by rebuilding their civilisation around a permanent gravitational fact created
by their own decision.

From then onward, Blackhole philosophy became obsessed with horizons.

The point after which returning to the old state is no longer possible.

### Territory / Home Systems

The original singularity still exists.

Around it grew Mawheart: nested cities, foundries and orbital structures arranged in precise
gravitational layers.

Every generation adds another ring.

At the centre remains the mistake.

They refuse to hide it.

### Titan Lineage

The Blackhole Titan is almost literal cosmology.

Its armour is midnight blue and black.
Orange-gold plasma burns along the edges like an accretion disc.
The entire ship is arranged around one central impossible darkness.

A singularity.

Everything curves toward it.

The surrounding hull forms four great hooked gravitational petals, giving the vessel the appearance
of a machine trying to contain a hole in reality.

Smaller Blackhole ships inherit:
central mass structures,
dark armour,
orange tidal glow,
curved claw-like hulls,
heavy reactors,
and geometry visibly organised around gravitational centres.

### Fleet DNA

MIDNIGHT ARMOUR · ORANGE ACCRETION FIRE · CENTRAL MASS · GRAVITATIONAL CLAWS

### Civilisation

Blackhole civilisation classifies actions as:

Orbital — easily reversible.

Escape — reversible only at serious cost.

Horizon — the old state cannot meaningfully be restored.

The law governs weapon deployment, machine fusion, planetary engineering and political authority.

Blackhole culture therefore appears cautious until a decision crosses the threshold.

After that, hesitation is considered dangerous.

### Economy / Trade

They dominate:
gravity engineering,
extreme reactors,
tidal mining,
plasma weapons,
void torpedoes,
and high-density capital construction.

### Warfare

Their fleets reshape the battlefield.

Gravity systems alter approach vectors.
Heavy ships hold space lighter craft cannot.
Enormous capacitors feed weapons for frighteningly long periods.

A Blackhole Titan does not need to chase everything.

It tries to make the battlefield come to it.

## Fleet Lineage — conceptual and future families

The Fleet Lineage from the original source contains future or conceptual archetypes not all present as runtime classes. Preserve the source spelling below; these are not claims that playable classes already exist.

### 00 — Embryonic Flight

Identity before fleet power

The opening end of the fleet is intentionally personal. These ships should feel vulnerable, readable and worth learning rather than disposable tutorial objects.

| Family | Design role | Source status |
|---|---|---|
| Pod | Emergency survival / personal transit | future |
| Starter Ship | First sovereign vessel | future |
| Frigate | Core light-combat platform | playable |

### 01 — Frigate Families

Specialisation begins

Frigate families establish the first meaningful doctrine splits: survivability, speed, support, interdiction, salvage and hunter-killer roles.

| Family | Design role | Source status |
|---|---|---|
| Assault Frigate | Armoured pressure frigate | future |
| Speciality Frigates | EWAR / scout / salvage / interdiction | future |
| Destroyer | Light fleet hunter / gun platform | playable |

The first true fleet backbone

Cruiser space is where faction identity should become unmistakable. Two cultures may both field a cruiser, but the problem each expects that cruiser to solve should be different.

|---|---|---|
