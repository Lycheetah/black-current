# Equipment progression: research and current evidence

This is a research record, not another backlog. Execution order is in ../PLAN.md.

CCP primary sources distinguish three useful sources of equipment depth:

- [Loyalty Points](https://support.eveonline.com/hc/en-us/articles/14141831188636-Loyalty-Points): missions and other activities earn corporation-specific rewards; LP stores include faction modules.
- [DED Complexes](https://support.eveonline.com/hc/en-us/articles/203269661-DED-Complexes): rated combat exploration is the source of deadspace module variants.
- [Mutaplasmids](https://support.eveonline.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000848365-Mutaplasmids): mutations have variable positive/negative effects; resulting modules show their changes and are unique.

For Black Current, this suggests affordable ordinary equipment, targeted faction
rewards, special encounters for rare variants, and bounded stat variation. Random
outer-station stock is Mac’s proposed game design, not a claim about EVE’s market.
Keep basic rookie stock available; persist rotated stock rather than rerolling
on menu open. One Equipment Codex should index discovered base models and traits,
using the existing modular inventory. These codex/market features are future work.

The old source had several overlapping equipment paths: every knocked-off part
at danger one (25% beyond it), a separate 35% surviving-part kill roll, 30% elite/
outpost special-fitting chance, and boss guarantees. These constants do not
constitute a measured overall drop rate.

The published 2.10.1 lowers ordinary break and kill paths while retaining boss
and promised rewards. Its browser sample records 10 normal equipment drops in
100 kills and 27 total drops in 100 elites; all four bosses supplied equipment.
This is one seeded kill-route sample. Actual parts knocked off in a complete
phone run, player income and upgrades per ten minutes still need measurement.
No additional currency or mandatory research layer is proposed.
